My academic interests

As a prospective computational linguist, my research interest falls in the niche between performance/corpus-compatible logical frameworks for linguistic analysis such as HPSG, LFG and categorial grammar and computational analyses in natural language processing, both in more traditional machine learning and data science as well as the more nascent advancements in deep learning, whose successes in natural language generation and comprehension have spurred great curiosity to their utility in linguistic analysis. Whether or not modern deep learning methods are suitable for large-scale modeling of the biological reality of human language processing, I believe that they have the potential to assist in the following ways:

  • Grammar induction may allow for an account of how symbolic generalizations in human language could theoretically emerge from input
  • As language models are trained on massive amounts of corpus data, they may capture certain cross-linguistic generalizations previously unaccounted for by more traditional corpus analyses
  • On the other hand, generalizations previously accounted for but not represented by language models may reveal where human linguistic competence may differ from their linguistic ability

The place of the aforementioned logical frameworks is to provide a rigorous, unified explanatory framework for linguistic analysis. Moreover, as word and sentence embeddings show some sort of an approximation of symbolic composition and generalization, such frameworks could allow for fine-grained, detailed analyses of these approximations, and conversely the continuous vector-based nature of word/sentence embeddings could allow for more fine-grained analysis that could assist in formulating more lexically oriented models of formal semantics.

Guide to the website

This site is a receptacle for all of my personal projects, both academic and not. I plan on putting up reports about projects of mine and update logs, writings about my interests, as well as opinion pieces on academia. My projects can all be found on my Github page, @codexderelict. My site is divided up into four subsections:

Linguistics, Math, Computers

This is where I put up articles related to academia, including code reports, surveys of the landscape of computational linguistics research, and generally anything that relates to linguistics, mathematics and computation.

Video Games, Music, Recreation

This is where I put up articles related to non-academic interests I have. These will mainly be reviews of games, shows, albums, et cetera.
