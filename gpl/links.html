Links

Academia

Math3ma
The website of Tai-Danae Bradley, category theorist with an interest in AI and language modeling. I recommend her "Language, Statistics and Category Theory" series!
ACL Anthology
Official site of the Association for Computational Linguistics, housing tons of papers. My research interests lie in SCiL, SIGMOL, and BriGap, check those out!
DELPH-IN
A suite of programs for the Head-driven Phrase Structure Grammar formalism, including the Linguistic Knowledge Builder and English Resource Grammar!
Fluid Construction Grammar
A formalized variant of Construction Grammar that focuses on language acquisition and change; implemented in Common Lisp!
LINGUIST List
A hub for academic linguists, filled with information about linguistic research!
World Atlas of Linguistic Structures
A website conglomerating all of the interesting syntactic and morphological features of languages all over the world!
Diversity Linguistics Comment
The blog of typologist Martin Haspelmath, one of the maintainers of the WALS, and his opinions!
General Ontology for Linguistic Description
A formalization of descriptive linguistics terminology.
Lexical-Realizational Functional Grammar
A constraint-based morphosyntactic formalism melding realizational morphology and Lexical-Functional Grammar, with a great ability to handle oligosynthetic and polysynthetic languages!
forall x: An Introduction to Formal Logic (on the Open Logic Project website)
A free introduction to formal logic, taking you all the way from propositional/truth-functional logic to proving soundness and functional completeness!
Open Logic Project
If you've finished/outgrown forall x, Open Logic Project also offers a free intermediate-advanced logic book too, taking you from set theory through model theory, incompleteness, lambda calculus, computability theory, and more!
